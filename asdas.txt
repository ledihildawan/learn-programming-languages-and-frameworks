10 Principle for Good Design

- Good design is innovative
- Good design makes a product useful
- Good design is aesthetic
- Good design makes a product understandable
- Good design is unobtrusive
- Good design is honest
- Good design is long-lasting
- Good design is through down to the last detail
- Good design is environmentally-friendly
- Good design is as little design as possible

For good UX Design, you should follow these practices:

- The product should be easily usable, logical, and self-explanatory
- The product should solve the user's problem
- The product should be accessable and usable to a wide range of people
- The product should create a positive experience 

Grid and Layout

For Desktop

- Columns: 12
- Rows
- Gutters: 12, 14, 16
- Margins: 160, 180

Most screen sizes are divisible by 8, so with the 8pt rule, all the dimensions of elements must be a multiple of 8, like 16, 24, 32, 40, and so on. This rule will create consistency in design but also reduce implementation time.

For Mobile

- Columns: 4
- Rows
- Gutters: 16
- Margins: 16, 20, 24

Typography

Line height inversely scales with font size: small text uses a multiplier of 1.6 (e.g. 16pt becomes ~26pt), while large text (>32pt) works best with 1.3 or 1.1. Optimal line length balances readability—too short makes text choppy; too long strains the reader’s eye.

Letter spacing, usually 0 by default, improves readability by adjusting gaps between letters—minor kerning may be needed for adjacent capitals, though most fonts follow well-calibrated design guidelines.
